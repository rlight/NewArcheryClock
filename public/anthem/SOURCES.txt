The Star-Spangled Banner — recordings used by the clock's National Anthem button.
All four are public domain as works of the U.S. federal government (U.S. Navy Band, U.S. Army Field Band,
U.S. Air Force Band), downloaded from Wikimedia Commons. Sources, performers and lengths are in anthems.json.

To add another anthem or version: put the audio file (mp3, m4a, ogg/oga or wav) in this folder and add an
entry to anthems.json (id, title, performer, file, durationSec). It appears on the control page automatically.
